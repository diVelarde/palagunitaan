# Review Process Changes

## Updated review steps

1. A contributor submits an entry, which enters the pending review queue.
2. The reviewer checks the entry and its sources. The reviewer is never the submitter; this rule applies to validators and administrators.
3. If the reviewer is the entry's submitter, the review is blocked and another reviewer must decide.
4. An eligible reviewer approves, disputes, or rejects the entry.
5. The system records the decision and notifies the contributor. Administrators may still delete inappropriate entries as moderation; deletion is not a review decision.

## Manuscript paragraph

To preserve independent review, no reviewer may make a decision on an entry they submitted. This restriction applies equally to validators and administrators: administrators are also blocked from approving, disputing, or rejecting their own entries, and another eligible reviewer must decide.
